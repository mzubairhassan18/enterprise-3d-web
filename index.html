frame 1 at the gate
loading the town…

Walk into the society.

Eye height, just outside the gate. The town rises around you as you scroll, forward or in reverse.

The gate lets you in

The barrier lifts and both leaves swing open beneath the board that reads “Welcome to Kashmir Town.” Then the asphalt draws itself east ahead of your feet, centre line and kerbs included.

The first house builds itself

Look left. The walls slide in, the roof drops from ten units and lands with a bounce, the door swings on its hinge. Then the fence pops up picket by picket and two pines grow out of the yard.

The same house, one block east

Glance left again. The second house runs the identical build on its own delay, so the street never assembles twice at the same moment.

The street walks itself

Pedestrians pace both sidewalks and turn at the ends of their own stretch, on a clock that ignores your scrollbar.

The roundabout takes root

The island rises out of the ground first, ring, kerb and grass, then the fountain stacks its basin, pedestal and bowl on top.

Water over the rim

The basin fills, a column lifts through the bowl, and the water breaks over the rim as a falling curtain, poured and unpoured by your scroll.

The mosque rises last

Past the water the hall lifts out of the ground, dome, glowing windows and door, then the minaret extends off the roof with its balcony, gold cap and spire.

The whole society

The camera cranes up and back until the plan lies under it: gate, road, three houses, roundabout, mosque. Everything you watched rise now holds still in one frame.
