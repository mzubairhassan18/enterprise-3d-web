Welcome to Kashmir Town.

A gated neighborhood where family homes, landscaped streets and everyday amenities sit behind one staffed entrance.

Streets designed for daily life

Kashmir Town's main avenue offers broad sidewalks, clear curbs and a painted center line, while a family home stands ready across the road.

A home built with care

Brick walls, a shingled roof and a front door that swings wide welcome the family inside. A picket fence and two pines finish the garden.

A second home, the same standard

One block east, the next residence follows the identical plan and the same craftsmanship, so the street reads as one considered address.

A neighborhood made for walking

Broad, level sidewalks carry neighbors in both directions, and the street plan puts homes, greenspace and daily errands within a short stroll.

A fountain at the crossroads

At the center of town, the roundabout gathers a clipped lawn and a tiered fountain basin, a landmark for the whole neighborhood.

The heart of the community

Water breaks over the fountain's rim as the mosque rises beyond it, its domed hall and glowing windows in place. It brings neighbors together a short walk from every door.

The minaret completes the skyline

The minaret extends above the roof with its balcony and gilded cap, while neighbors go about their day on the sidewalks below.

Room to grow

Past the roundabout, Kashmir Town marks its edge with a boundary wall left open where future phases will connect, so today's plan can grow tomorrow.

A new quarter rises

Three five-story apartment residences take shape together, alongside a hospital marked by its red cross and a neighborhood bank. Home, care and daily banking all sit within a short walk.

The master plan from above

From above, the gate, three homes, roundabout, mosque, hospital and the new quarter read as one plan. Open land waits at the edges, ready for whatever Kashmir Town becomes next.
